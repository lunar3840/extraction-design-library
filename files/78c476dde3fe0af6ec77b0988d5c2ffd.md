## Sheet 01

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: architectural design exploration board for a top-down extraction videogame, not a screenshot, not a map and not an implementation blueprint. Setting: an abandoned, partially flooded municipal machinery-maintenance district, reclaimed by machines; humans only pass through in small scavenging groups, no inhabited base or civilian life. Industrial infrastructure from a believable twentieth-century city. Semi-realistic stylized game concept art: convincing material depth, bevels, wall thickness, construction joints and weathering, controlled painterly textures and slightly simplified readable forms; preserve a subtle cartoon-rendered game aesthetic, NOT Minecraft, voxel, toy low-poly, photoreal photograph or cyberpunk neon. Shared restrained palette of weathered masonry, concrete, rust, desaturated enamel and sparse greenery. Clear diffuse daylight, crisp architecture, no dramatic fog. Structures must look clearly DIFFERENT in silhouette, roof section, height rhythm, opening proportions, wall material and projecting/recessed volumes. Closed opaque roofs except intentional abandoned damage; don't expose whole interiors in every image. Ground-level usable entrances and a clearly unobstructed apron with two onward walking routes, no decorative walls across the doors. Architecture is the focus, not piles of unrelated props. No people, no robots, no occupied camps, no cars obscuring the facade, no shipping-container main buildings. Some dormant machine-retrofit fittings are appropriate on power/control facilities. Show real construction logic: supported awnings, walls with depth, roofs bearing on walls, feet meeting ground. Do not reproduce any current repetitive white-brick warehouse facade. This is an original new visual direction.
