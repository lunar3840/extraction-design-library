# after07 independent static visual review

Read-only inspection of all 18 `captures/after07` shots, matched to `captures/before04` on the latest CLINIC02 map checkpoint and compared with `captures/after06` for size. The after07 manifest reports `status=complete`, `completedShots=18/18`. The shader change was a 2x depth-scaled area envelope; the tested camera poses, C# geometry selection, and renderer fix stayed the same. No Unity or production files were edited for this review. Motion, blend, and functional verification are separate gates.

## Main result

The extra area is now visible as nearby environment rather than only the actor. W03, S11, W04, E08, POWER, HALL, and PARTS all have more useful space than after06. The former perfect round mask has not returned. The reveal takes its main direction from the building/roof and is softly blended at the perimeter. HALL remains localized and no longer exposes the whole roof or street. PARTS no longer ghosts the broad roof/facade. POWER retains an actual opening; its after04 actor-only failure is resolved. Actor cores remain legible in the reviewed frames.

There is a visible limitation: broad flat roofs still give the opened area a straight-sided quadrilateral or trapezoid impression. In HALL outside the **outer fade perimeter** runs diagonally across otherwise intact roof tiles; in PARTS and POWER it similarly bounds a translucent patch over the roof. These are distinct from real wall, shelf, awning, and roof-panel edges visible inside the opening. W03 also shows a long diagonal transition across roof material. The result is more natural than a closed circle or the old unbounded wedge, but it is not an exact contour of every roof detail and should not be described as such. Still images do not establish whether a thin 1-pixel synthetic outline is present; a broad translucent/geometry boundary remains the main visible shape.

## Verdict and remaining gate

As a first building-aligned area revision, after07 is **conditionally acceptable on static appearance**: the player has useful nearby visibility, the original artificial closed circle is gone, and large accidental building reveals stay contained. The remaining straight-sided perimeter on flat roofs is a meaningful known limitation, not a fully solved natural-edge look. Before final acceptance, inspect root's motion and blend captures for edge/width switching, depth breathing, opacity pulses, enemy silhouette/health-bar rules, shadows and lighting, and camera-specific outline-mask cleanup. A motion artifact or renewed roof-wide ghosting would override this static verdict.
