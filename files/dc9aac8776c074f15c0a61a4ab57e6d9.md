## Independent checks

- Contact points are compared against the nearest **actual triangle**, including under rotated and nonuniform source scale. A joined convex corner must carry at least one ordered path across adjacent faces in a deterministic 24-seed sweep. A four-strip wall leaves a real window hole. Every ordered path segment gets eight intermediate surface support and exclusion samples. Stem edges and full leaf geometry are checked for gap/box intrusion. A glass-named front surface must be ignored while the opaque wall is kept.
- Exact vertex/triangle/UV and contact hashes test repeatability, changes to seed/density, and no disturbance of `UnityEngine.Random.state`. Segment, leaf, and triangle limits are checked.
- The real car prefab references RustyCarsFree mesh data with imported `isReadable: 0` (`Assets/OlegWER/RustyCarsFree/FBX/fbx.fbx.meta` in the checked local project copy). The harness asserts the selected car still contains at least one non-readable mesh, then builds against it and a representative ruin home prefab. It independently acquires the car's source triangle data and checks sampled output contacts within 1.5 cm of actual triangles. Source references, hierarchy, materials, transform/collider serialization, and asset dependency hashes are compared before and after.
- An injected cancellation stops an in-progress build and checks that no unsaved output mesh remains. Authoring checks generated collider count, regeneration root count, unrelated-child preservation, and baked mesh/material references after synchronous reload.
