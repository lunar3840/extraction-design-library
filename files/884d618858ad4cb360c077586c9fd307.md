## Code observations

- The cue clones player meshes at runtime and uses its own material. The source renderers, source materials, and hair materials are not modified by the cue source.
- The cue activates only when the nearest camera-to-low-player ray hit is a matching 1F/2F façade collider; it does not reveal the player through arbitrary interior cover. The hall resets the global exterior veil in `OnDisable`.
- `HitscanService` now passes the full `RaycastHit` to the hall pointer filter, allowing selective pointer skipping for visually removed outside roof/facade tiles while physical shot queries retain their colliders. The gameplay report verifies physical and pointer behavior under both the attached canopy and the outside south approach roof.
