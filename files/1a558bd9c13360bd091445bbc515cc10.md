## Implementation and verification

### Actual changes and method

- Replaced the full inventory's old health card/bar, weight caption and visible body-status pill with the accepted transparent strip. No footer fill, border, blur or panel shadow is drawn. Black shadows apply only to text/icons.
- Four original Figma icons, exported at 4× resolution, render at 20px: weight, health, water and energy. Existing Noto Regular/Bold are reused; Medium is derived at weight 500 from the same original Noto Sans KR variable font.
- Current values remain prominent at 26px; maximum and change sit at their right at 12px. Existing Figma layout scaling is used, including a bounded fit for unusually long real numbers.
- Values come from the live inventory/player: carried weight and allowance, effective health maximum, and the current rule maxima for water/energy. Low needs/health and excess weight use pastel red.
- Added observation properties to `PlayerState` without changing consumption/recovery calculations: signed natural water/energy rates and actual clamped recovery totals. Natural loss is red per minute; received recovery is pastel blue for three seconds and has no per-minute suffix. The UI polls while hidden and rebinds its baseline on player changes.
- Clicking the health metric opens the existing body-details pane. Character presentation, equipment layout, rigs, backpacks, item use and saves are preserved.
