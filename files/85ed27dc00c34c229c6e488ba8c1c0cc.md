# Vehicle ruin lining staging handoff

- Scope: a copy of the reusable geometry engine plus `VehicleLiningBuilder.cs` under this staging folder. No production Unity asset, original FBX, material, building model or scene was modified.
- Vehicle method: collect only polygons removed by the existing cut from original opaque render triangles, then keep panels near the vehicle's outer envelope with outward-facing normals. The helper moves each panel through one inward world-space field: about 0.025–0.050 m at the torn edge, gradually deepening toward 0.160–0.230 m inside the damage volume, with a small continuous crease. A distinct dark iron substrate and cut-boundary strips form the lining. It does not fan-fill a perimeter or create polygons where the original source had no triangles. World positions are quantized to 1 mm to suppress duplicate source faces and duplicate boundary strips.
- Small-source-mesh issue: backing/stitch face normals are computed from world-space coordinates using double precision; faces with world cross-product squared area at or below 1e-14 are omitted. Stitch winding and normals face consistently away from the cut center. Existing Building/Prop `AddRimRibbons` and clipping output paths remain unchanged.
- Generated contract: `__RuinGenerated_VehicleBacking_<renderer>` child beneath the tool-owned generated root, material `RuinGenerated_ironBacking`; report fields `vehicleBackingTriangles`, `vehicleBackingMeshes`, `vehicleBackingMinRecessWorld`, `vehicleBackingMaxRecessWorld`, `vehicleStitchTriangles`. Stitch triangles also count toward `capTriangles` and the final triangle budget.
- Failure policy: existing rollback restores source renderers and discards backing meshes/materials and report counters. Original source meshes/materials remain untouched; collision remains a separate bake requirement.
- Verification so far: standalone C# compile against Unity 6000.1.0f1 CoreModule and this project's Unity.Collections succeeded with zero warnings/errors. Unity vehicle rendering, finite/nondegenerate mesh validation, seed/budget, save/reopen, and building fingerprint equality are pending the integration and independent review gates.
