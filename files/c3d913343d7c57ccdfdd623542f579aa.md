I opened all 12 `main/visual_review/BUILDINGS_01.jpg`–`BUILDINGS_12.jpg` comparison sheets. They contain the 68 building exterior and interior before/after pairs from the same fixed editor cameras. I also opened full-resolution C01, E02, N17, S01, HALL and W10 images to inspect material assignments and room/detail shape.

- Across these fixed views, the building-specific roof and exterior palettes remain recognizable; no obvious wholesale gray replacement, mirrored facade, missing shell, or lost roof is visible.

- The N17 tall wall and grass patch are also present in the matching original view. They are not evidence of a new Blender regression.
- HALL's first after Editor interior camera showed a higher outer window band than the before interior camera, although the exterior view remained similar. I subsequently opened `after_play/after_building_HALL_interior.png`: the upper band is hidden and the lower walls match the original cutaway composition. This supports a stale HallV13 Editor visibility cache as the cause of the first image difference. The actual Play image resolves the visible cutaway concern; final roof behavior still depends on the explicit Play validation report.

These contact sheets establish broad visual continuity at their scale. They do not prove door traversal, loot interaction, cutaway behavior in Play, topology, or performance. The independent Editor, Play, and performance reports remain required before final acceptance.
