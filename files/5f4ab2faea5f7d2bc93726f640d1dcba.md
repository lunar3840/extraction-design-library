Primary request: Design THREE distinct human raider enemy classes, with completely concealing gas masks so no facial or eye animation is needed. Create a premium polished original game concept-art board. They are adult human survivors in the ruined industrial world, wearing scavenged human protective work clothing and firearms. Three LARGE isolated FULL BODY characters, head through soles visible, ample clear space between silhouettes. Each occupies approximately one third of the canvas, same baseline and camera angle, no overlapping equipment. Small restrained typography only: at upper left "ANIMA" and subtitle "HUMAN RAIDERS / CONCEPT 01"; below figures left "01 SCAVENGER", center "02 BREACHER", right "03 SUPPRESSOR". A tiny palette strip at lower right.
Style/medium: sophisticated anime game production design, clean decisive contours, subtly painterly cel shading, physically legible matte cloth, rubber, scratched metal and smoky glass. Compact SD / super-deformed proportions matching a tactical game with large-headed 3.5-to-4-head-tall characters, short sturdy limbs and oversized readable hands and boots. These are visibly adult fighters represented in SD, not children. Serious alert poses. Not realistic eight-head adults. Not plastic toys. No body exaggeration or pinup presentation.
Shared design language: entire face and scalp hidden by sealed black rubber full-face respirator and fabric hood; TWO SLANTED broad opaque smoke-amber lenses separated by a thick central bridge, no visible pupils or skin. Wide cheek-mounted low-profile rectangular pleated filter cartridges, short central exhale grille, no dangling breathing hoses. Restrained amber lens REFLECTION, never light emission. Scavenged rainproof work jackets, cloth gloves, cargo trousers, heavy practical boots, webbing, rugged external loot bags and a few patched armor plates. Soft seams and ordinary human joints remain clearly visible. Faded slate-teal and charcoal fabric, aged gray or bone armor, ochre maintenance tape and a short dirty rust-orange neck wrap give shared identity. Restrained accent colors occupying under 15 percent; no neon. Shoulder and backpack-top ochre patches are visible from a top-down view. Weathering localized to hems, kneepads, corners, not uniformly muddy. No ornate logos or faction names, no spikes, no skulls.

No floating ammo belts. No exposed mechanical limb joints.

Production-ready design clarity over dramatic fog. Include clean natural hand anatomy, two arms and two legs per human, boots fully in frame.

Avoid copying Girls' Frontline faction uniforms, insignia or specific characters; the sole high-level inspiration is anonymous masked human enemies. No purple scarves, no glowing red round goggles. No readable microtext, no watermark, no UI, no extra portraits on this lineup.
