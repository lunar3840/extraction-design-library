A temporary 1 mm threshold caused a false new failure in diagnostic 02 and was removed. The integration owner approved **only the two greater-than-2 cm current approach points** as candidate-specific NavMesh resampling. `BuildExpansionContract.Run()` compares each against the frozen diagnostic and fixed measured position within 0.1 mm, requires the unchanged fields, and records the diagnostic `current` entry in `allowedLootChanges`. It rejects additional entries outside the original 2 cm threshold or drift beyond the approved positions. `StageABlenderExpansionValidation.Run()` separately checks both declared positions against the actual candidate within 0.1 mm. This does not establish interaction success: final PlayerMotor walking and `LootInteractor.Open` in Play remain required gates.
