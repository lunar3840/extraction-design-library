# Surface Ivy refinement: independent validation

Status: **candidate 03 final code and isolated demonstration reviewed; validation note frozen**. Unity was run by the main agent only. This is a follow-up to `a-map-surface-ivy-tool`. The user asked to refine leaf form and distribution, not to place ivy across A-map. Only the validation-owned staging copy and this note were edited by the validation agent.

## Baseline harness fit

- The copied `SurfaceIvyValidation.cs` matches the prior baseline. It does not assume the old seven-vertex default leaf. Mesh checks iterate actual vertex and triangle arrays, and the prior limits compare leaf count with `settings.maxLeaves`.
- The deterministic output hash includes generated vertices, triangle indices and UVs. The window test checks attachment paths, stem/leaf triangles and all leaf vertices against its exclusion volume. The corner, non-readable wreck, cancel, source preservation and Bake/reload checks remain relevant after the growth change.
- The staged profile has 25 vertices and 24 fan triangles per default leaf. At the settings hard cap of 12,000 leaves, that implies up to 300,000 leaf vertices and 288,000 leaf triangles before stems. It is a finite geometry ceiling, not a frame-rate result. The validation copy asserts a per-default-leaf mesh ceiling and checks all three source profiles directly at neutral and control extremes; `CORE_183527.txt` reports these checks passed in Unity.
- Static review of `staging/leaves/SurfaceIvyLeafProfile.cs`: it exports the specified API and counts, uses monotonically increasing polar angle for a counter-clockwise boundary, clamps x/y radius, and bounds the lifted z. This supports the planned finite/winding checks. Whether the three variants read as distinct ivy leaves in the actual camera is an art question for the new renders.
- Static review of `staging/growth/SurfaceIvyGenerator.cs` while the growth agent is editing: public `Build` parameters and deterministic private RNG remain; triangle collection, surface support, exclusions and cancellation are still called. New root spacing, per-path length, occupancy, top-rim and leaf run/gap logic may affect the old corner/window tests, so those tests remain unchanged as regression gates. This is a source review, not a passing Unity execution result.

## Scope of evidence

- The final review below uses six matched before/after image pairs and one additional car close view. It is an art assessment of isolated demonstration assets, while the geometry tests are a separate functional result. Map placement, Play behavior and FPS are outside this refinement validation.

## Actual candidate 01 validation and art review

`CORE_182304.txt` reports PASS for the new three-profile structural group and all five retained core groups. This includes finite/star-shaped/CCW profile geometry, deterministic output, hole and exclusion clearance, rotated/nonuniform corner behavior, cancellation cleanup, a non-readable real car mesh and source-reference preservation. `COMMAND_core_182304.txt` shows the open A-map scene stayed `dirty=False` and Play stayed off. This is a functional pass for the current integrated source, not an art pass.

I opened all six pairs from `baseline_renders/` and `candidate_01/`. The `CAMERAS.txt` files match position, rotation, orthographic size and aspect pairwise. Both render reports use the same settings recipes and original source hashes; all three candidates report zero new colliders. The generated leaf count dropped from 1,149 to 314 on the car, 2,622 to 934 on the shell, and 2,010 to 797 on the roof. These counts explain the changed density; they are not frame-rate measurements. Candidate triangle totals reconcile exactly as 10 triangles per stem segment plus 24 triangles per leaf (for example car: 374×10 + 314×24 = 11,276), confirming that the new fan profile is actually wired into the generated geometry.

- **Car:** The prior front bumper/bonnet green mass is gone and rusted metal is legible again. Candidate 01 is now mostly a few long exposed stems over the bonnet, windshield edge and rear. Leaves look small and rounded at the recorded camera scale; the mature/young/five-lobed silhouettes are not separately recognisable. Wide bare areas and the wire-like front strand make the growth feel sparse rather than naturally clustered. Next tuning should restore local leaf groups near selected body contours while keeping open metal and glass visible.
- **Shell:** The almost continuous green top-rim band is broken, which is an improvement. The new pattern still places many small, evenly spaced points along upper edges, with fewer substantial downward clusters. A few stronger hanging pockets around corners and masonry breaks, with clear spans between them, would read better than continuous rim decoration or isolated dots. Window/door openings remain visually legible in the two recorded views.
- **Roof:** The large broken opening remains uncovered, but top and oblique views show thin wandering green lines with too few visible leaf masses. Compared with the baseline, the roof now reads more like drawn paths than overgrowth. Concentrated patches around selected edges or cracks and larger visible leaf silhouettes would help while preserving the clear roof hole.

Candidate 01 is worth retaining as an intermediate because it removed the previous blanket and rim stripe. It is **not the final visual result** for the user's request. The next pass should be judged from new matched views and, if possible, a close view of leaf silhouettes; structural profile success does not prove visible lobes at game camera distance.

## Candidate 02: visual review and corner regression investigation

`candidate_02/REPORT.txt` records isolated car, shell and roof renders with the same settings recipes/source hashes and zero new colliders. Its six standard camera entries match the baseline camera parameters; `after_car_detail.png` adds a closer leaf view. I opened all seven candidate images. This set shows the **pre-isolation code state** that produced `CORE_182841.txt`, where the convex-corner seed sweep failed. It is valid art comparison evidence for that state, not final-code functional evidence.

- Car: paired leaf groups are now visible on the bonnet, front fender, door/side and rear, with more rusted body showing than in the original blanket. Compared with candidate 01, the vines read less like bare wires. The close image shows leaf vein shading and a softer lobed outline rather than the old hard folded-paper appearance. Individual three- and five-lobed variants are still subtle at the normal isometric and top camera distances. Some isolated long stems remain, but the overall balance is better.
- Shell: more substantial hanging leaf groups appear around selected wall corners and breaks, while gaps interrupt the previous continuous upper-rim band. The outer and inner wall materials, doors and windows remain visually legible in these views. The pattern still favors wall tops, so the next evaluation should check whether lower wall patches are sufficiently varied.
- Roof: leaf chains and small clustered patches are more visible than candidate 01's thin lines. The large missing opening remains clear in the rendered views. A few long routes still read as linework in top view; this is an art limit, not a demonstrated surface-hole failure.

Functional timeline: `CORE_182841.txt` passed profile, determinism, window/exclusion, cancel and real-asset groups but failed the 24-seed convex-corner assertion. After separating leaf-style RNG from path RNG **and** extending the same-path occupancy adjacency allowance, `CORE_183140.txt` passed all groups, with `COMMAND_core_183140.txt` showing Play off and the open scene clean before and after. That result alone did not isolate which of the two changes restored the corner crossing. Extra paired leaves consume more random draws when style and growth share one RNG, plausibly changing branch/heading choices. The short same-path occupancy allowance can also stop a valid path that turns tightly around a corner, and a longer allowance could hide self-crowding in another shape. The unchanged 24-seed corner test remained the gate; both prior logs were preserved.

The controlled follow-up resolved that ambiguity for this regression: `CORE_183342.txt` passed with leaf-style RNG separated **and the original 3-step same-path adjacency rule**, and `CORE_183527.txt` passed after removing the temporary widened-rule field/comment and restoring the original `path.steps - old.step < 3` condition verbatim. Both commands report Play off and `dirty=False` before/after. The final staged source retains RNG separation and **does not widen** the occupancy exemption. This supports extra shared-RNG consumption from paired leaf styling as the proximate reason the earlier 24-seed corner sweep lost its crossing. It does not imply the 24-seed test proves every corner or all seed values.

## Candidate 03: final independent review (frozen)

I opened the six `baseline_renders/before_*.png` images and all seven `candidate_03/after_*.png` images. For every paired iso/top view, `CAMERAS.txt` records identical position, rotation, orthographic size and aspect; the extra car detail view has no baseline mate and is used only to judge leaf form. The baseline and final `REPORT.txt` files record identical recipes and original source hashes for each object. The final isolated demonstrations have no new colliders and produced 308 segments / 412 leaves / 12,968 generated triangles on the car, 1,051 / 1,414 / 44,446 on the shell, and 1,080 / 1,458 / 45,792 on the roof. The triangle counts reconcile with 10 per stem segment plus 24 per leaf. These are mesh counts, not performance results.

- **Car:** The previous green blanket over the front fenders, bonnet and bumper is replaced by separated growth along the rear, roof opening, window pillar, side and a small bumper group. Rust, glass opening and body contours are legible. Paired leaves and visible veins in `after_car_detail.png` have a softer lobed ivy shape than the earlier folded/angular look. The almost straight pillar-to-roof vine still looks somewhat arranged, and three- versus five-lobed varieties remain subtle at the standard camera distance. This is a clear distribution and close-up form improvement, not a claim of final vegetation art polish.
- **Shell:** The previous near-continuous ivy ribbon along the wall top breaks into separated groups, with larger hanging clusters around selected corners and wall breaks. Wall materials and most door/window shapes remain legible in both views. Some upper-rim preference remains, but the intermittent growth reads less like uniform trim.
- **Roof:** The large broken opening remains visually clear and the surface is not carpeted in leaves. The final growth is more selective than the baseline. At these normal camera scales, many routes still read as thin green lines; leaf variants are not readily distinguished. Visual clearance in two views cannot establish clearance for every hidden opening or camera; the separate geometry tests cover their designed cases.

`CORE_183527.txt` reports PASS for all six groups: profile structure, deterministic seed/density/budgets/source invariance, window and exclusion clearance, rotated/nonuniform corner, cancel cleanup, and real non-readable car plus structural prefab. `AUTHORING_183647.txt` reports PASS for regenerate, unrelated-child preservation and Bake/reload persistence. `COMMAND_after_183553.txt` and `COMMAND_authoring_183647.txt` show Play off and the open A-map scene `dirty=False` before/after. `PROTECTED_FINAL.json` reports all 11 protected files, including the A-map scene and map prefab, with identical expected/actual SHA-256 hashes. The three clean art demonstration prefab paths are in `candidate_03/DEMO_PREFABS.txt`; the authoring test's saved car prefab is a separate persistence specimen with an unrelated test child.

**Conclusion:** Candidate 03 satisfies the requested modest refinement in the isolated car, shell and roof demonstrations and passes the available functional and persistence checks. The remaining thin-line roof appearance and weak distant distinction between leaf variants are recorded art limits. This review does not establish A-map placement, in-game Play appearance, frame rate or exhaustive geometry coverage. No further source or validation changes are proposed in this frozen review.
